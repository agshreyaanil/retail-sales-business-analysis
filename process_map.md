## Overview
This document outlines the As-Is (current state) and To-Be (future state) process for retail sales and inventory reporting. The gap between these two states forms the basis for the requirements defined in the BRD.

---

## As-Is Process (Current State)

### Description
Currently, store managers collect sales data manually from individual store systems at the end of each month. Data is consolidated into separate Excel files per region and emailed to the Finance Director. There is no standardized template, no automated validation, and no central visibility across regions. The Finance Director manually merges files and builds ad hoc reports, which takes 3–4 business days each month.

### Pain Points Identified
- No single source of truth for sales data
- Manual consolidation is error-prone and time-consuming
- Regional managers cannot see their own performance without requesting a report
- Discount decisions are made without visibility into profit impact
- No early warning system for underperforming categories or sub-categories
